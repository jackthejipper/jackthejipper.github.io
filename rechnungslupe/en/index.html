Rechnungslupe – Help

Rechnungslupe makes e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X) readable – on the Mac right in Finder with the space bar, on iPhone and iPad via “Share”. Buy it once in the App Store, use it on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Your invoices stay on your device.

  • Mac, iPhone and iPad
  • One-time purchase, no subscription
  • No account, no tracking

Invoices often contain bank details and data about other people. Only send us an invoice file if you are allowed to – a description or a screenshot without bank details is often enough. How we handle your message is described in the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • Mac with macOS 15 or later; iPhone with iOS 18 or later; iPad with iPadOS 18 or later
  • Languages: German and English – the app follows your system language
  • Formats: XRechnung (UBL and CII, including credit notes and corrected invoices), ZUGFeRD from version 2.0.1 and Factur-X (PDF with embedded invoice data), Peppol BIS Billing 3.0
  • Files up to 10 MB
  • No account and no internet connection required

Purchase and devices

Buy once, use on all your devices

Rechnungslupe is a one-time purchase in the App Store, with no subscription. One purchase covers Mac, iPhone and iPad: sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account on each device and download Rechnungslupe there without paying again.

Restore a purchase, new device

Rechnungslupe doesn’t need a separate restore button – the purchase belongs to your Apple Account. On the new device, open the App Store, sign in with the Apple Account you used for the purchase and download the app again. You’ll find your purchased apps in the App Store under your profile (iPhone and iPad: tap your profile picture; Mac: click your name in the bottom-left corner).

Refunds

Apple handles App Store purchases, including refunds; as the developer, we cannot issue a refund. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account and request a refund there.

Frequently asked questions

The space bar doesn’t show a readable invoice in Finder (Mac)
  1. Open Rechnungslupe once so that macOS registers the preview extension.
  2. Turn on the extension: choose “No preview? Enable the extension…” in the welcome window, or turn on Rechnungslupe under “Quick Look” in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.

No XRechnung at hand? In the welcome window, “Save a Sample File to Try It…” saves the sample invoice and shows it in Finder – then just press the space bar.

The space bar preview works for XRechnung files (XML). For ZUGFeRD and Factur-X PDFs, Finder shows its normal PDF preview; you see the embedded invoice data when you open the file with Rechnungslupe. XML files that are not invoices are shown as text.

Finder icons don’t show the seller and amount (Mac)

macOS only picks up a newly installed icon extension after you log out and back in or restart. Until then, Finder shows the usual XML icon. Seller and amount appear only for XRechnung files (XML).

How do I open an invoice on iPhone or iPad?

Tap the invoice in Mail or the Files app and choose “Share” › Rechnungslupe. The Files app shows XML files with its own text preview, so “Share” is the way into the app. Alternatively, choose “Open Invoice…” in Rechnungslupe (several at once go into the invoice collection). On iPad you can also drag and drop files into the app.

“Not an e-invoice” – what does that mean?

The file contains no invoice data that Rechnungslupe can read, e.g. an ordinary PDF without an embedded XRechnung (“Regular PDF, not an e-invoice”) or some other XML file. “Show XML” shows the content of an XML file, “Open Another File…” lets you pick a different one. Rechnungslupe reports PDFs in the ZUGFeRD 1.0 format as outdated: since 2025, only invoices from ZUGFeRD 2.0.1 onwards count as e-invoices in Germany.

Rechnungslupe shows a notice or an error in the check

Rechnungslupe recalculates line totals, VAT, total with VAT and amount due according to the EN 16931 business rules and checks important mandatory information. A notice does not automatically mean the invoice is invalid – the standard allows small rounding differences, for example. For real calculation errors, contact the seller. The check does not replace a full validation such as the one performed by the KoSIT validator.

Why is there no GiroCode?

Rechnungslupe only creates the EPC QR code for bank transfers if the invoice is in euros, is paid by credit transfer (not direct debit), contains a valid IBAN and a payee, and has an amount to pay.

Reminders don’t work
  • Rechnungslupe needs permission for the Reminders app. On the Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Reminders; on iPhone and iPad: Settings › Apps › Rechnungslupe › Reminders.
  • The Reminders app needs a list; Rechnungslupe uses your default list.
  • The reminder alerts you at 9:00 AM on the day of the deadline. If there is already an open reminder for the same invoice on the same day, Rechnungslupe does not create a second one.

Several invoices at once (invoice collection)

On the Mac, open the collection via File › Invoice Collection (⇧⌘2) and drag files in; on iPhone and iPad, you find it on the home screen. The collection shows the total, check status and due dates; duplicate invoices (same seller, number and amount) are marked “Duplicate” and are not counted in the total. The CSV export matches your Excel (with a German region: semicolon and decimal comma; otherwise comma and decimal point).

If you open several invoices from Finder with “Open With”, they appear in separate windows. “Add Open Invoices” moves them into the collection.

Finder services and shortcuts
  • In Finder: right-click an invoice › Services › “Save as PDF with Rechnungslupe” or “Open in Invoice Collection”. If the items are missing, turn them on in System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts… › Services.
  • In the Shortcuts app on Mac, iPhone and iPad: “E-Invoice as PDF” and “Check E-Invoice” – also with Siri, e.g. “Check e-invoice with Rechnungslupe”.

Does Rechnungslupe need an account or the internet?

No. Rechnungslupe works without signing in and without an internet connection. The app reads your invoices on your device and sends nothing over the internet.

Privacy

Rechnungslupe reads your invoices on your device – no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app sends no data to us or to third parties. Details are in the privacy policy.
