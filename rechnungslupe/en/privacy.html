Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy applies to the app Rechnungslupe for Mac, iPhone and iPad and to this website (jackthejipper.github.io). It describes which data is processed – and which is not. This is a translation of the German version; if the two differ, the German version prevails.

Summary

  • Rechnungslupe reads your invoices only on your device. The app, the space bar preview and the Finder icons send nothing over the internet.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app contains no third-party libraries or SDKs, only components from Apple.
  • We receive no data about you through the app. The only exception: crash reports that Apple passes on to app developers if you have allowed this in your device settings (section 4).
  • Apple handles the purchase through the App Store. We do not learn your name or your payment details.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other providers. When you visit it, the hosting provider GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address (section 5).

2. The app: processing on your device only

2.1 Viewing invoices

When you open a file, Rechnungslupe reads it on your device, recalculates it and displays the invoice. PDFs, the CSV export and the GiroCode (EPC QR code for bank transfers) are also created on the device. The app does not change your files. If a file is stored in iCloud Drive or with another storage provider, the system may download it for this purpose – through your account with that provider, not through Rechnungslupe.

For some features the app temporarily places copies in its temporary folder: opened attachments of an invoice, PDFs for sharing, and files dropped onto the app on iPad. The app deletes these copies the next time it starts.

If you open an invoice on iPhone or iPad via “Share” from another app, such as Mail, and Rechnungslupe cannot open the file in place, iOS puts a copy into the app’s “Inbox” folder. Rechnungslupe does not delete these copies itself; they remain on the device until you delete the app. The list of recently opened invoices (section 2.3) may point to such copies.

2.2 Space bar preview and file icons

The extensions for the preview (Quick Look) and for file icons read only the file the system wants to display. They store nothing and make no network connections; on the Mac they do not even have permission to do so. Like for other files, the system may keep generated icons in its own cache.

2.3 Settings and recently opened invoices

The app’s settings on your device contain:

  • your preferences, e.g. whether the welcome window appears at launch, and on the Mac whether the drop magnifier is shown and where;
  • on iPhone and iPad, the list of recently opened invoices (at most 12): the file’s location with an access bookmark, seller, invoice number and amount due. You can remove entries in the app.

On the Mac, macOS itself keeps the list of recently used documents; the welcome window shows the latest entries from it.

Rechnungslupe does not send this data off the device. Like all app data, it may be included in backups you have set up yourself, such as an iCloud backup or Time Machine.

2.4 Reminders

Only when you choose “Remind Me” does Rechnungslupe create a reminder for the discount deadline or the due date in Apple’s Reminders app. The system asks for your permission once. The reminder contains the seller, amount, invoice number and date, IBAN, BIC, payee and remittance information; on the Mac also a link to the invoice file. To avoid duplicates, the app first reads the title and first note line of open reminders due on the same day – on the device only.

The reminder is stored in your Reminders app. Whether it syncs with your other devices via iCloud depends on your Apple settings; Rechnungslupe does not transfer it. You can revoke access at any time: on the Mac in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Reminders, on iPhone and iPad in Settings › Apps › Rechnungslupe.

2.5 Shortcuts, Siri and Finder services

The shortcuts “E-Invoice as PDF” and “Check E-Invoice” and the Finder services “Save as PDF with Rechnungslupe” and “Open in Invoice Collection” work entirely on the device. If you run a shortcut with Siri, Apple processes your voice input under its own terms; Rechnungslupe only receives the command and the files you pass to it.

2.6 Printing, sharing, copying and links

When you print an invoice (including via AirPrint), pass it on via “Share” or copy values to the clipboard, this uses system features. Whatever you pass on to other apps, printers or services is processed by them under their own terms.

Links in the app open your browser, and only when you tap or click them: “Help & Support” and “Privacy Policy” lead to this website (section 5). If an invoice refers to an external attachment, “Open Link” opens the address stated in the invoice; the terms of the respective provider apply there.

2.7 Permissions

  • Files: Rechnungslupe only reads files that you open yourself, drag into the app or pass to it via “Share”. On the Mac, the app runs in Apple’s App Sandbox.
  • Reminders: only with your permission and only when you choose “Remind Me” (section 2.4).
  • Internet: none. The app and its extensions make no internet connections of their own; on the Mac they do not even have permission to do so.

3. Purchase through the App Store

You buy and download Rechnungslupe in Apple’s App Store. Apple handles purchase, payment, download and updates under its own responsibility; Apple’s Privacy Policy applies. We only receive aggregated sales reports from Apple, such as the number of purchases per country – no names, email addresses or payment details.

If you rate or review Rechnungslupe in the App Store, we – like everyone else – see the name you chose and your text and can reply publicly.

4. Crash reports and app analytics via Apple

If you have allowed your device to share analytics data with app developers, Apple provides us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics, such as the number of active devices. According to Apple, this data does not identify you personally. We use it solely to find and fix errors in the app.

You can change this setting at any time: on the Mac in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › “Share with app developers”, on iPhone and iPad in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › “Share With App Developers”.

5. This website

5.1 Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser transmits technically necessary data to GitHub’s servers, in particular your IP address, date and time, the requested address and information about your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, it logs the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes. We do not receive these logs and do not analyze visitor data.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: we have a legitimate interest in providing help, the privacy policy and the legal notice for the app securely and reliably. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). More details, including how long GitHub keeps data, are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5.2 No cookies, no third-party content

This website sets no cookies and uses neither JavaScript nor analytics or tracking services. It loads no content from other providers, such as fonts, scripts or videos. There is no contact form. Links to other websites, such as Apple’s, open their pages; their privacy policies apply there.

6. Contact by email

When you email us, we process your email address, your name if you provide it, and the content of your message including attachments in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request concerns the use of the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in answering requests. You are not obliged to give us any data; without an email address, however, we cannot reply.

We receive and store emails to this address using Apple’s iCloud Mail service.

Invoices often contain bank details and data about other people. So please only send us an invoice file if you are allowed to – a description or a screenshot without bank details is often enough.

7. Legal bases at a glance

  • Processing on your device (section 2): We do not receive this data; the app processes it on your device, under your control. Storing and accessing information on your device – settings, the list of recently opened invoices, temporary copies – is strictly necessary for the app to provide the features you have expressly requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). Rechnungslupe only accesses the Reminders app with your permission in the system dialog.
  • Purchase and reviews (section 3): Apple is responsible for purchase and payment. The aggregated sales reports we receive do not allow any conclusions about you. We read and reply to reviews on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in feedback on the app.
  • Crash reports (section 4): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in fixing errors in the app. You allow Apple to pass them on to us in your device settings.
  • Website (section 5): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Email (section 6): Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

There is no automated individual decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

8. Retention

  • On your device: temporary copies until the next start of the app; copies that iOS places in the “Inbox” folder when opening via “Share”, until you delete the app; settings and the list of recently opened invoices, until you remove them or delete the app. On the Mac, the settings remain in the app’s data folder after you delete the app (in your home folder under Library › Containers, shown in Finder as “Rechnungslupe”) until you remove it. Reminders remain in the Reminders app until you delete them there.
  • Emails: until your request has been dealt with; longer only where statutory retention obligations apply, for example for business correspondence under German commercial and tax law, or as long as necessary to resolve claims.
  • Website server logs: at GitHub, as stated by GitHub (section 5.1).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). To exercise them, write to info@getinvoiceflow.de.

Right to object: You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR).

Data that Rechnungslupe stores only on your device is not held by us. You can delete it yourself by removing entries in the app or deleting the app (on the Mac, also its data folder, see section 8).
